# Share with the team

1. Read the selected material and choose the team from the private `8-System/team-context.md`. Verify repository identity and default-branch SHA using `docs/git-read.md` in the team clone. Read the affected pages, indexes and owners.
2. In the same session, use `.agents/skills/team-update/SKILL.md` from that team clone. Prepare self-contained shared material with context, sources and limitations on a team branch. Exclude private scratch notes and follow the private-sharing rules linked by `team-update`.
3. Show the author the exact publication scope and obtain any missing approval. After approval, continue `team-update` until a real Draft PR is created or updated, then return its URL. Do not require switching sessions or handing off a patch file.

Do not store a team patch or PR draft in the private brain. Respect explicit preview-only requests and report access blockers without claiming a PR exists. The private `8-System/team-context.md` path remains unchanged by the team's folder layout.
